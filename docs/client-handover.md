## What changed

- The gym system now runs **on this computer**. There are no more monthly hosting fees.
- **It works without internet.** Internet is only needed for the daily backup.
- All your members, registrations, payments and products came across from the old system.
- **A membership ends the day before the same date.** One month from 1 Oct ends on 31 Oct, and a renewal starts the next day. The end date can still be changed by hand.
- **Income figures are now the money actually received.** The old dashboard multiplied plan prices by the number of sign-ups, so its totals looked different.

## Opening it

Double-click **Lifestyle** on the desktop and type the password. Always use the same Windows account on this computer, because the app's data belongs to that account.

## Backups: what to look for

- The app copies everything to your Google Drive by itself, into a folder called **Lifestyle Backups**. It does this every day and when the app is closed.
- **Top bar, green "Backed up … ago"**: all good.
- **Orange or yellow message about backups**: check the internet is working, then press **Back up now**. If it still fails the next day, call for help.
- A copy is also kept on this computer every day for two weeks.

## Please don't

- Delete or move the **Lifestyle Backups** folder in Google Drive.
- Change the computer's **date, time or time zone** (it must be Sri Lanka time). Membership dates depend on it.
- Uninstall the app or sign in to Windows with a different account to use it.
- **Delete** registrations to tidy the lists. Use **Dismiss** instead; it hides them without losing history.

## If the computer breaks or is stolen

**Your data is safe in Google Drive.** Get any Windows 10 or 11 computer and call for help. It takes about 20 minutes to install the app and bring everything back, as of the last backup.

## Everyday tasks

| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Sign up a new member | **New registration** (top right) → plan → members → payment → save. The joining fee is added automatically for new members; untick it to waive it. Renewals don't add it, so tick it if someone should pay it again |
| Change a joining fee, or how long a break counts as re-joining | **Plans** → edit the plan's joining fee, or change the days at the top of the page |
| Renew someone | **Renewals due** or **Not renewed** → **Renew**, or click their name → **Renew** |
| Follow up on memberships ending this month | **Renewals due**: press **WhatsApp**, tick the packages to offer, then **Open WhatsApp** (or call). Then **Renew** or **Dismiss** |
| Win back people who stopped coming | **Not renewed**: press **WhatsApp**, tick the packages to offer, then **Open WhatsApp** (or call). Then **Renew** or **Dismiss** |
| Take money from someone who owes | Click their name → **…** on the registration → **Add payment** |
| Remind someone who owes | Click their name → **…** on the registration → **WhatsApp payment reminder** |
| Change the WhatsApp wording (renewals, balances, store credit), or which packages are ticked to start with | **Settings** → **WhatsApp reminders** → **Save messages** |
| Set up personal trainers | **Settings** → **Personal trainers** → **Add trainer**. Tick **This is the owner** for yourself (no commission); give each other trainer their commission % |
| Set up the personal training packages you sell | **Personal training** → **Session types** → **Add session type**: name, sessions, price, and how long they have to use them |
| Sell personal training | **Personal training** → **New package** (or **New package** in the member's details). Choose the member (they need a current membership), trainer and session type; the sessions, price, use-by date and commission fill in and can be changed. Enter what they paid now; the receipt opens |
| Personal training at check-in | Members with sessions left choose **Gym workout** or **Personal training with …** on the door tablet; personal training uses a session, and a notice pops up here. At the desk, **Check in** asks the same. Removing a check-in gives the session back |
| See trainers' commission | **Personal training** → **Commission**: each trainer's packages starting that month, what the member has paid, and the commission. **Sessions** shows who trained whom |
| Give someone longer to use their sessions | **Personal training** → **…** on their package → **Edit** → move **Use by** on |
| Sell a drink or supplement | **Store** → **Sell**. Choose the member under **Bought by** (leave it empty for a walk-in); it then shows in their details |
| Let a member pay later | When selling, choose **On credit** and the member (walk-ins can't). Nothing is paid yet, so it isn't in the takings until they pay |
| See who owes for store items, and remind them | **On credit**: everyone who owes, how much and for what. **WhatsApp** opens a reminder listing the items. The member's details show it too |
| Take money for store items on credit | **On credit** → **Take payment** (or **Take payment** in the member's details). Any amount up to what they owe; it pays off the oldest items first, and the receipt shows what's left |
| Fix a credit sale entered by mistake | **On credit** → **Credit sales** → bin icon. It comes off what they owe and the items go back into stock |
| Record a delivery, or correct stock after counting the shelf | **Store** → **Stock** on the product → **Stock arrived** or **Counted the shelf**. **Store → Stock changes** lists every change and who made it |
| Let an instructor sell or update stock on their phone | **Settings** → **Instructor phones** → **Pair a phone**, type their name, then they scan the code with their phone on the gym Wi-Fi. Their sales show under **Store → Sales** with their name, and a notice pops up here as they happen |
| Stop a phone (lost, or the instructor left) | **Settings** → **Instructor phones** → bin icon next to their name. Their past sales keep their name |
| Give a receipt | It opens after every payment: press **WhatsApp** or **Print**. For an older payment: **Payments** → receipt icon on its row |
| Get the accounts for a period (e.g. for the accountant or tax) | **Payments** → **Accounts report** → choose the period (e.g. **Last tax year**, 1 April to 31 March) → **Save Excel file**. It has every payment with its receipt number, income by type and payment method, takings per month and per day, what was owed at the end, store sales and stock, memberships and personal training sold, and trainer commission. Members appear by name only |
| See the day's takings | **Payments** → **By day**: cash, card and bank transfer for each day. Click a day to see its payments; the WhatsApp button sends that day's summary to the owner |
| Check the gym from your phone (owner) | Open the **Lifestyle daily summary** sheet Google emailed you, in the Google Sheets app. It updates every hour while the app is open, and when it's closed at night |
| Find a member | Type their name, phone number or NIC in the search box at the top |
| List registrations by plan or status (e.g. everyone on One Month who has expired) | **Registrations** → choose a status and a plan; **Owing money** narrows it to unpaid ones |
| See who came in today | **Check-ins**. Members check in on the door tablet; check someone in at the desk with **Check in a member** |
| Pair a new door tablet | **Settings** → **Door check-in tablet** → **Pair a tablet**, then scan the code with the tablet |
| See how the month is going | **Dashboard**: income, active members, registrations and renewals compared with last month, and charts of the last 12 months (**Table** shows the numbers). The arrows at the top right go to other months. For any other dates, use **Payments** |
| Switch to dark mode (easier in the evening) | **Settings** → **Appearance** → **Dark** (or **Light**). It's remembered on this computer |
| Merge people who were entered twice | **Members** → **Possible duplicates** |

WhatsApp messages are typed in for you but **not sent**: read the message, then press send in WhatsApp.
